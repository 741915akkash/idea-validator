📄 FILE: Interviews (Concept)
🎤 Interviews

Interviews are how you collect real-world evidence for your idea.

🎯 Purpose

To replace assumptions with:

Observations
Conversations
Actual user input
🧠 How Interviews Fit
Checkpoints → define what you need to validate
Interviews → gather real data
Revisions → update answers
Score → reflects improved understanding
⚠️ Key Principle

If you don’t run interviews, your confidence score is based on assumptions.

🔁 Types of Interviews
Quick Interviews
Fast capture
Unstructured
Used during or after conversations
Structured Interviews
Guided
Aligned with checkpoints
More systematic validation
💡 Outcome

Interviews turn:
👉 Ideas → Evidence
