📄 FILE 7: Revisions (Concept Page)
🔁 What are Revisions?

A revision is a new version of your idea after updating answers.

🎯 Purpose

To:

Track how your thinking evolves
Prevent random changes
Enable structured improvement
🧠 Why Revisions Matter

Without revisions:

Changes are invisible
Progress is unclear

With revisions:
👉 You see how learning impacts your idea
