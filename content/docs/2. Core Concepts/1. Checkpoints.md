📄 FILE 3: What is a Checkpoint
🧩 What is a Checkpoint?

A checkpoint represents one critical aspect of your startup idea.

Instead of evaluating your idea as a whole, the system breaks it into smaller parts.

🎯 Purpose

Each checkpoint helps you:

Focus on one dimension of your idea
Identify specific risks
Avoid vague thinking
🧠 Examples of What Checkpoints Cover
Problem frequency
Severity of the problem
Evidence of existence
Market discussion
Feasibility
⚠️ Why This Matters

Most founders think in broad terms:

“This is a good idea”

Checkpoints force you to answer:
👉 Which part is strong? Which part is weak?

🔁 How It Fits in the System
Overview → shows all checkpoints
Checkpoint screen → where you answer questions
Score → aggregates results across checkpoints
