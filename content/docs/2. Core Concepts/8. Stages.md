🧭 Stages

Stages represent where a lead is in your process.

🎯 Purpose

To track progress from:

Initial contact
→ to
Qualified interest
→ to
Decision or conversion
🧩 Example Stages
New Lead
Qualified
Proposal Sent
Negotiation
🧠 How It Works

Each lead belongs to a stage.

As interactions happen:
👉 You move the lead forward

⚠️ Why This Matters

Without stages:

You lose track of progress
Follow-ups become random

With stages:
👉 You see exactly where each lead stands

💡 Outcome

Stages turn:
👉 Contacts → structured pipeline
