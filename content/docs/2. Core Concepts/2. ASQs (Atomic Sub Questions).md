🔬 ASQs (Atomic Sub-Questions)

ASQs break down complex questions into smaller, concrete parts.

🎯 Purpose

To help you:

Avoid vague answers
Think in specifics
Answer difficult questions step-by-step
🧠 When to Use ASQs

Use them when:

A question feels unclear
You’re unsure how to answer
You’re guessing
💡 Example

Instead of answering:

“How often does this problem occur?”

ASQs guide you with:

Did it happen in last 7 days?
How many times last week?
Was it consecutive?
⚠️ Why This Matters

Without ASQs:

Users guess
Answers become unreliable

With ASQs:
👉 Answers become grounded in reality
