📊 Market Score vs Confidence Score

Your idea is evaluated on two dimensions.

🎯 Market Score

Measures:

Strength of the problem
Demand
Frequency and severity
🎯 Confidence Score

Measures:

Quality of evidence
Reliability of answers
Consistency across responses
🧠 How to Interpret
Scenario	Meaning
High Market + High Confidence	Strong idea
High Market + Low Confidence	Needs validation
Low Market + High Confidence	Likely weak idea
Low Market + Low Confidence	Unclear
⚠️ Key Insight

A good idea with weak evidence is still risky.
