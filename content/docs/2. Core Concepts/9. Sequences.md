🔁 Sequences

Sequences define how you follow up with leads over time.

🎯 Purpose

To:

Ensure consistent outreach
Avoid missed follow-ups
Systematize communication
🧠 How It Fits
Leads → who to contact
Sequences → how to contact
Responses → improve your idea
🧩 Structure

A sequence consists of:

Multiple steps
Each step includes:
Action (call, email, note)
Timing (day-based)
⚠️ Important

Sequences don’t replace thinking.

👉 They ensure you execute consistently

💡 Outcome

Sequences turn:
👉 Leads → active conversations
