📄 FILE 5: Notes
📝 Notes

Notes allow you to capture your thinking alongside each answer.

🎯 Purpose

To:

Record assumptions
Capture examples
Track uncertainties
Store insights
🧠 How to Use Notes

Write things like:

“Observed this in 2 interviews”
“Not sure, need to validate”
“Assuming based on personal experience”
🔁 Editable Anytime
Notes are saved
You can revisit and update them
They evolve as your understanding improves
💡 Why Notes Matter

Answers alone don’t show reasoning.

Notes help you:
👉 Understand why you chose an answer
