📊 CRM — Manage and Engage Leads

The CRM helps you track and engage the people connected to your idea.

🎯 Purpose

To:

Organize leads
Track progress
Manage follow-ups
🧠 How It Fits in the System
Interviews → discover people
Leads → track them
Stages → track progress
Sequences → follow up
🧩 What You Can Do
Add and manage leads
Move leads across stages
View leads in table or pipeline format
Assign follow-up sequences
⚠️ Important

This is not a generic CRM.

👉 It is tied to your idea validation process

💡 Outcome

CRM turns:
👉 Validated people → structured pipeline
