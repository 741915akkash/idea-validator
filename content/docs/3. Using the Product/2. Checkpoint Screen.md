To validate a specific part of your startup with structured questions.

Questions

Test assumptions like:

Problem frequency
Impact
Evidence
ASQs (Atomic Sub Questions)

Help you think clearly
Notes
Capture thoughts, assumptions, and examples
Saved and editable anytime
⚠️ How to Answer

Avoid:

Guessing
Optimistic answers

Prefer:

Evidence
Real examples
