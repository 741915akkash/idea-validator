📄 FILE 1: What This Product Does
🧠 Idea Validation, Not Guesswork

Most founders build based on assumptions.

This leads to:

Solving problems that don’t matter
Targeting the wrong users
Wasting time on weak ideas

This system helps you:

👉 Break your idea into critical parts
👉 Test each part systematically
👉 Replace assumptions with clarity

🎯 What You Get
A structured way to evaluate your idea
Clear visibility into risks and weak areas
A repeatable process to improve your thinking
⚠️ What This Is NOT

This is not:

A quiz to “approve” your idea
A tool to confirm your beliefs

This is:
👉 A system to challenge and refine your thinking

🧠 Core Principle

Strong ideas come from validated understanding, not intuition.
