🧠 Validate Your Idea

This workflow helps you turn a rough idea into a clear, testable, and improvable concept.

🔁 The Full Loop
Start at Overview
Complete checkpoints
Score your idea
Identify weaknesses
Create a revision
Improve answers
Repeat
1️⃣ Overview → Your Roadmap
9 checkpoints
Each represents a key part of your startup
Shows progress and gaps
2️⃣ Checkpoints → Deep Thinking

Each checkpoint:

Tests one aspect of your idea
Forces you to answer structured questions

Use:

ASQs → to break down thinking
Notes → to capture assumptions
3️⃣ Score → Decision Engine

Once complete:

Market Score → strength of idea
Confidence Score → reliability of answers
Decision → what to do next
4️⃣ Revision → Improve Thinking
Start a new version
Update answers based on new insights
Re-score to see impact
5️⃣ History → Track Progress
Compare versions
See what changed
Understand why scores moved
🎯 Outcome

You don’t just get a score.

You get:
👉 A clear path to improve your idea
